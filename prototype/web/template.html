Real, and proven so

  • The traversability cost function — every term and threshold.
  • The safety supervisor — all nine stop conditions, in evaluation order.
  • The semantic class vocabulary, imported rather than copied.
  • Only the supervisor's output ever reaches the wheels.
  • check_parity.py compares the ported logic against the shipping C++ on 8,000 cases, including every threshold boundary. They agree exactly.

Stand-ins

  • A raycast over a height field, in place of a stereo camera and GPU elevation mapping.
  • A* over the cost grid with an inflation layer, in place of Nav2 and its predictive controller.
  • Terrain classes are read from the map rather than detected by a model.

Not shown — do not claim it

  • Visual SLAM is not modelled. The vehicle knows exactly where it is. This demonstrates terrain reasoning and the safety gate, not that GPS-denied localisation works — that needs the full stack and trajectory error measured against ground truth.
  • No physics, no wheel slip, no image formation.
